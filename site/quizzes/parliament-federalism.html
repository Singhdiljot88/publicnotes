🏛️ Quiz: Parliament & federalism

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. How many elected seats does the Lok Sabha have?

    The Lok Sabha has 543 elected seats; 272 is the majority mark. Read Note 021 →

  2. Q2. Under which article is the Union Budget presented as the Annual Financial Statement?

    Article 112 covers the Annual Financial Statement, usually presented on 1 February. Read Note 023 →

  3. Q3. Which body recommends how taxes are shared between the Centre and states every five years?

    The Finance Commission under Article 280 recommends tax-sharing. Read Note 026 →

  4. Q4. On which Census have inter-state seat allocations been frozen?

    Seat allocation between states has been frozen on 1971 Census figures. Read Note 028 →

  5. Q5. Until which year did India hold Lok Sabha and state assembly polls together?

    Simultaneous elections were the norm until 1967. Read Note 030 →
