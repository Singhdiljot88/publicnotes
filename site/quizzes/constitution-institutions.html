📜 Quiz: Constitution & institutions

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. Which part of the Constitution contains enforceable Fundamental Rights?

    Fundamental Rights are in Part III and can be enforced in court, though most are subject to reasonable restrictions. Read Note 001 →

  2. Q2. Which 1973 case held that Parliament cannot alter the basic structure of the Constitution?

    Kesavananda Bharati (1973) created the basic structure doctrine. Read Note 004 →

  3. Q3. Under Article 74, what happens after the President asks the Council of Ministers to reconsider its advice once?

    Ministerial advice is binding after a single reconsideration request. Read Note 005 →

  4. Q4. Which amendments gave constitutional status to panchayats and municipalities?

    The 73rd Amendment covers panchayats; the 74th covers municipalities. Read Note 009 →

  5. Q5. What does the CAG do with its audit findings?

    The CAG audits public accounts and reports to legislatures; it does not itself prosecute or punish. Read Note 008 →
