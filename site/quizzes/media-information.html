📰 Quiz: Media & information

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. Which article is the basis of press freedom in India?

    Press freedom flows from Article 19(1)(a), subject to restrictions in Article 19(2). Read Note 051 →

  2. Q2. What are TRPs based on?

    TRPs estimate viewing from a sample of metered homes. Read Note 052 →

  3. Q3. In 2024, what did fact-checkers report about election misinformation?

    Older tactics still dominated, even as a smaller number of AI-generated claims circulated. Read Note 054 →

  4. Q4. If a viral claim cannot be verified quickly, what should you do?

    Unverified claims should not be shared as fact. Read Note 055 →

  5. Q5. Which body runs Doordarshan and All India Radio?

    Prasar Bharati, in existence from 1997, runs both. Read Note 058 →
