🗳️ Quiz: Election Commission & voter rolls

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. Which form is used for a new inclusion in the electoral roll?

    Form 6 is for inclusion, Form 7 for objection or deletion, and Form 8 for correction or shifting. Read Note 012 →

  2. Q2. What is SIR?

    Special Intensive Revision is a deep re-verification of rolls ending in a draft, claims and objections, and a final roll. Read Note 013 →

  3. Q3. Under Article 324(5), how can a Chief Election Commissioner be removed?

    Removal needs proved misbehaviour or incapacity, an inquiry, a special majority in each House in the same session, then a presidential order. Read Note 018 →

  4. Q4. What legal power did the 31 October 2026 symbolic public impeachment have?

    The event was a political demand; only the parliamentary process can remove a CEC. Read Note 017 →

  5. Q5. According to the notes, what does falling public confidence in the ECI in surveys measure?

    Survey confidence measures perception, not proof of fraud. Read Note 020 →
