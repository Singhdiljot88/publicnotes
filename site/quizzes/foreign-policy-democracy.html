🌏 Quiz: Foreign policy & democracy

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. Strategic autonomy grew out of which earlier policy?

    It grew out of non-alignment and is now often described as multi-alignment. Read Note 081 →

  2. Q2. In which year did the Galwan Valley clash take place?

    The clash happened in June 2020. Read Note 082 →

  3. Q3. What was India's operation after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack called?

    Operation Sindoor began on 7 May 2025. Read Note 083 →

  4. Q4. Which right does an OCI card NOT give?

    OCI holders cannot vote or hold constitutional posts. Read Note 087 →

  5. Q5. Does the Chief of Defence Staff command the three Service Chiefs?

    The CDS, created in 2019, does not exercise military command over the Service Chiefs. Read Note 090 →
