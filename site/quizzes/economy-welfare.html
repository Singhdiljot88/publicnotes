🌾 Quiz: Economy & welfare

Five questions drawn from the ten notes in this theme. Tap an answer to check it; each answer links to the note that explains it.

  1. Q1. Under MGNREGA as originally enacted, how many days of work could a rural household demand each year?

    MGNREGA guaranteed up to 100 days; its 2025 replacement sets a headline of 125 days. Read Note 072 →

  2. Q2. What share of the rural population can the National Food Security Act cover?

    The NFSA covers up to 75% of the rural and 50% of the urban population. Read Note 073 →

  3. Q3. How much cashless hospital cover does Ayushman Bharat–PM-JAY offer per family per year?

    PM-JAY offers Rs 5 lakh per family per year. Read Note 074 →

  4. Q4. Since September 2025, what are GST's two main slabs?

    GST was simplified to 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate for select sin and luxury goods. Read Note 077 →

  5. Q5. Which body recommends the Minimum Support Price?

    MSP is announced by the government on the recommendation of the CACP. Read Note 079 →
