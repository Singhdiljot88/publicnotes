Not the book, just the chronology — plus Chapter 1 and Sports, in full, for the website.

This is a set of original Public Notes on India: a chronology, plus two full notes on General Information and Sports, written in original words.

All notes

1 · Ancient India — to about 1200 CE

Harappan cities, Vedic texts, Mauryas, Guptas, Cholas — 10 timeline entries.

2 · Medieval India — about 1200 to 1707

Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Deccan sultanates, Mughals, Marathas — 10 entries.

Plassey to Partition, through revolt, reform and mass movements — 14 entries.

4 · Post-Independence India — 1947 to 2000

Constitution, wars, Emergency, liberalisation — 12 entries.

5 · India Today — 2000 to 2026

Digital growth, welfare rights, reforms, and the politics of the present — 11 entries.

6 · Themes Across Time

Science, culture, saints and reformers, defence, sport, geography and economy — traced across the whole timeline.

India at a glance: shape, states, records, national symbols — with pictures and corrected book errors.

8 · Sports — Chapter 30

Olympics, Asian and Commonwealth Games, sport by sport, stadiums — updated to Paris 2024, with pictures.

How to use it

  • Read the chronology in order for the story, or jump to an era for revision.
  • Use Themes Across Time to revise by subject.
  • Use General Information as the one-page foundation, and Sports for the Games and stadiums.

Corrections carried into these notes

  • Bharat Ratna, not 'Bharat Ratan' as printed in places in the book.
  • Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) is the highest peak wholly within India; K-2 (Godwin-Austen) lies in territory claimed by India but administered by Pakistan.
  • Olympic total: 41 medals after Paris 2024 (10 gold, 10 silver, 21 bronze), not the book's 35 to Tokyo.
  • Men's hockey: 13 medals (8 gold, 1 silver, 4 bronze), not 12.
  • Book population (142.7 crore) and forest-cover (24.62% forest + tree cover) figures are dated to the book's edition — see the General Information note.

Coverage and gaps

  • 57 chronology entries across 5 eras, plus a themes page, plus 2 full chapter notes.
  • Ancient/Puranic dates are traditional/approximate; regional histories are summarised, not exhaustive.
  • Post-2024 'today' entries age quickly and need re-checking at publish time.
  • Pictures: 4 hotlinked Commons images in total (3 in General Information, 1 in Sports). No raster India map and no Sindhu portrait are embedded — no confirmed-licence files were available for those, and no image was invented. The Neeraj Chopra Word-file picture is not hotlinked because its exact Commons filename was not stated in the Word caption.
  • Not live: this set is website-ready locally only. It has not been pushed to GitHub, deployed to Vercel, or added to the live publicnotes.org site.
