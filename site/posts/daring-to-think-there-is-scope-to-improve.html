Daring to Think There Is Scope to Improve

A note in my own words, started 1 November 2026 · 1 min read

People will frown upon these notes for daring to compare the country, the regime, and democracy in action.

People like me are watching it in awe.

For daring to think that there is a scope of movement to improve doesn't hurt me.

Watching in awe is not attacking the country. It is watching because I think it can move, because I think there is scope to improve.

A democracy that cannot be questioned in public is not being protected by silence. It is being left unexamined.

Daring to think that doesn't hurt me. And it doesn't hurt the country either.
