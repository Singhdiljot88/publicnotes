Union territories and the special case of Delhi

Note 129 of 300 · 2 min read · Fact-checked 2 November 2026

Union territories are administered by the President through an administrator, under Article 239. There are eight union territories: Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (merged in 2020), Lakshadweep, Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu and Kashmir, and Ladakh. Three of them, Delhi, Puducherry and Jammu and Kashmir, have elected legislatures and councils of ministers.

Delhi has a special status under Article 239AA, added by the 69th Amendment in 1991. It is the National Capital Territory with a Legislative Assembly that can make laws on state and concurrent subjects, except public order, police and land, which remain with the Union. The Lieutenant Governor (LG) acts on the aid and advice of the council of ministers, but can refer differences to the President.

Disputes between Delhi's elected government and the LG reached the Supreme Court repeatedly. In 2018, a Constitution bench held that the LG is bound by the council of ministers' advice except on reserved subjects, and cannot act independently on every matter. In May 2023, another Constitution bench held that the Delhi government has legislative and executive control over services, the bureaucracy, except in the reserved fields. Within days, the Union government issued an ordinance, later passed as the Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi (Amendment) Act, 2023, creating a National Capital Civil Service Authority in which the Chief Minister is outnumbered by two senior officials, with the LG having the final say. The Act's validity has been challenged.

Jammu and Kashmir became a union territory with a legislature in 2019, and elections were held in 2024; demands for the restoration of statehood continue. Ladakh, a union territory without a legislature, has seen protests demanding statehood and Sixth Schedule protections.

Key takeaways

  • There are eight union territories; Delhi, Puducherry and Jammu and Kashmir have legislatures.
  • Article 239AA reserves public order, police and land in Delhi for the Union.
  • The 2023 Delhi services verdict was followed by a law giving the LG the final say over services.

Why it matters for UPSC: Union territories, especially Delhi's governance disputes, are a frequent GS II topic.

Practice question: Analyse the conflict between the elected government of Delhi and the Lieutenant Governor in the light of recent judgments.
