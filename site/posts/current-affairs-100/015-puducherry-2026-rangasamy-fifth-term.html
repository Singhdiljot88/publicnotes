Puducherry 2026: Rangasamy's fifth term and how a UT chief minister is appointed

Note 015 of 100 · 1 min read · Fact-checked 2 November 2026

The NDA retained power in the Union Territory of Puducherry in 2026, winning 18 of the 30 elected seats. N. Rangasamy's All India N.R. Congress (AINRC) won 12 and the BJP 4. On the opposition side, the DMK won 5 and the Congress just 1. Rangasamy won both seats he contested.

He was then sworn in for a record fifth term as chief minister. The appointment process differs from a state's, which is why it is worth knowing. In a state, the governor appoints the chief minister under Article 164. In Puducherry, the Government of Union Territories Act, 1963, provides that the chief minister is appointed by the President, and other ministers are appointed by the President on the chief minister's advice. Accordingly, reports said President Droupadi Murmu appointed Rangasamy before the Lieutenant Governor administered the oath.

Puducherry is one of three Union Territories with a legislature, along with Delhi and Jammu and Kashmir. Article 239A allows Parliament to create a legislature and council of ministers for Puducherry. Its assembly can also include up to three members nominated by the Union government, a provision upheld by the Supreme Court in 2018 and often contested in close houses.

Key takeaways

  • The NDA won 18 of 30 seats; the AINRC won 12 and the BJP 4.
  • N. Rangasamy began a record fifth term as chief minister in May 2026.
  • A Puducherry chief minister is appointed by the President under the 1963 Act, not by the Lieutenant Governor.

Why it matters for UPSC: The difference between a state and a UT with a legislature is a frequent prelims topic.

Discussion question: Compare the constitutional position of the chief minister of Puducherry with that of a chief minister of a state.
