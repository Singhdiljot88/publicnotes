Assam 2026: the BJP wins a majority on its own

Note 014 of 100 · 1 min read · Fact-checked 2 November 2026

In Assam, the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) won a third straight term, taking 102 of the 126 seats. The BJP alone won 82, its first majority on its own in the state, with a vote share of 38.59%. Its allies, the Bodoland People's Front (BPF) and the Asom Gana Parishad (AGP), won 10 seats each. The Congress won 21.

Himanta Biswa Sarma, who became chief minister in 2021, was sworn in for a second consecutive term on 12 May 2026. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union Home Minister Amit Shah attended the ceremony, along with other NDA leaders, according to reports.

The return of the BPF as an NDA partner is notable. The BPF dominates the Bodoland Territorial Region, a Sixth Schedule area governed partly by its own territorial council under the 2020 Bodo peace accord. Its alliance with the BJP brought the Bodo vote back into the ruling coalition.

The election was also the first in Assam after the 2023 delimitation of assembly and Lok Sabha seats in the state. That exercise, carried out by the Election Commission on the basis of the 2001 Census, redrew constituency boundaries while keeping the total at 126; it was criticised by opposition parties at the time.

Key takeaways

  • The NDA won 102 of 126 seats; the BJP alone won 82, its first solo majority in Assam.
  • Himanta Biswa Sarma was sworn in for a second term on 12 May 2026.
  • Allies BPF and AGP won 10 seats each; the Congress won 21.

Why it matters for UPSC: Assam combines several syllabus themes: Sixth Schedule areas, the Bodo accord and delimitation.

Discussion question: How have peace accords and Sixth Schedule arrangements shaped electoral politics in Assam?
