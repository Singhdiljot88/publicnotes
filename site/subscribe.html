Get new notes

Two ways to follow Public Notes. Neither needs an account.

📬 By email

Send one email and you are on the list. Every Sunday you get a short note: three new entries, one from the archive, and one question to think about. It is sent by hand from my own inbox, with everyone in BCC, so your address is never shared or sold. Reply "stop" at any time.

Email me to join

📡 By RSS

Use any feed reader (for example Feedly, Inoreader or NetNewsWire) and add this address:

https://publicnotes.org/feed.xml

Open the feed

✍️ Write back

There are no comments here, on purpose. If you spot an error, want a topic covered, or use these notes in a class, email me. Corrections and reader questions are collected in a monthly note, credited by first name.
