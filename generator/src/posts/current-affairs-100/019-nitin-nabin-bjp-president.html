Nitin Nabin becomes the BJP's youngest national president

Note 019 · Theme 2: Elections & states · Fact-checked 2 November 2026

On 20 January 2026, Nitin Nabin was declared elected as the national president of the Bharatiya Janata Party, succeeding J. P. Nadda. Born on 23 May 1980, he became the youngest person to lead the party. Reports said he was the only candidate and was elected unopposed.

Nabin is a five-time member of the Bihar assembly. He first won Patna West in a 2006 by-election, and then the Bankipur seat in 2010, 2015, 2020 and 2025. He served as a minister in the Bihar government, holding road construction and urban development, and resigned after being named the party's national working president in December 2025. Nadda had led the party since January 2020; his term was extended beyond its scheduled end because of the 2024 Lok Sabha election.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi described the organisational election as fully democratic. In March 2026, Nabin was also elected to the Rajya Sabha from Bihar.

Party organisation matters for UPSC because the Constitution says little about political parties. They are registered with the Election Commission under Section 29A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, and the Tenth Schedule (anti-defection law) is one of the few places they are named. Internal party democracy is not legally enforced; the Law Commission's 170th report (1999) and others have recommended regulating it.

Key takeaways

  • Nitin Nabin was elected BJP national president on 20 January 2026, succeeding J. P. Nadda.
  • He is the youngest person to hold the post and a five-time MLA from Bihar.
  • Parties are registered under Section 29A of the RP Act, 1951; internal democracy is not legally regulated.
